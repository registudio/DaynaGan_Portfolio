## Overview

Euna Air is Ecovolt Technologies' environmental monitoring and sensor-fusion solution. During my hardware engineering internship I owned its bring-up and customer deployment.

## What I did

- Owned the hardware bring-up and customer deployment of Euna Air, completing rigorous on-site validation at customer premises within one week.
- Developed embedded firmware and field-testing procedures, identifying discrepancies between laboratory simulations and real-world operating conditions to support reliable deployment.
